This site uses [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io) to count visits.
It's served from this domain rather than plausible.io, so you won't see a third-party request, but the data does go to Plausible.

What that means:

- No cookies, and nothing is stored on your device.
- No personal data is stored, and you aren't tracked across sites.
  Plausible uses your IP address and browser to tell visitors apart for a day, then throws that away.
  See [Plausible's data policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy) for the details.
- If your browser sends [Global Privacy Control](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/), the analytics script isn't loaded at all.
- The stats are public: [plausible.io/klercke.com](https://plausible.io/klercke.com).

I'm a paying Plausible customer with no other connection to them.
Thedescription above is my summary of how their service works; their data policy is the authoritative version.

Like any web server, mine keeps standard access logs (IP address, page, and browser) for a short time, for troubleshooting and abuse prevention.
